FARHOLD

Land on a world of a real star system, walk it to the horizon, kill what is out there, and wear what it drops. The other planets of the system are in the sky above you.

World settings
land on a settled, many-biomed world — about a quarter of seeds have none, and those step to the nearest seed that does

A region is one level band. Smaller regions mean the next band is a shorter walk away — the bands always tile the whole 1–30 range whichever you pick.

Round 4: the RPG expansion. Every region has its own level band, monsters come in packs with champions and rares in them, dungeons are real places you go inside, chests and bosses drop a haul, and everything you do not want recycles into crafting material.
