# Mech game brainstorm (owner rulings) — started 2026-11-17

Working notes until the game has its own folder; then this moves to `prototypes/<game>/docs/PLAN.md` §0.
Idea #3 from `docs/ideas-sprite-games.md`. Inspiration (chat only, never in game text): a fast mech series the owner
plays with a friend, Diablo-style loot, Warframe crits.

## Round 1 rulings (items 1–109 from chat)
- **Camera:** Arcline's iso angle. Q/E view turns confuse him — may keep, not essential.
- **Rendering:** player mechs live 3D (customizable, swappable parts). Owner asks: trash enemies prerendered, rival
  mechs / tougher ones live 3D? → yes, a mix.
- **Movement:** all yes (boost, quick boost, jump/hover, energy, heat bar, leg types, weight, lock-on, water) EXCEPT
  wall kick (odd with Arcline art). Functional parts beyond arms/legs: CPU, generator, cooling.
- **Building the mech:** all yes. Diablo-style loot; reuse Voidwright's loot roller (crafting rolls). Crafting + loot
  from kills + mission rewards. Do NOT flood rare loot early.
- **Weapons:** all yes. Overload weapon → a late special part (an "ultimate"). Wants a talent-tree-like system (e.g.
  two-handed weapon in one arm) but mech-themed, not a fantasy tree.
- **Enemies:** all OK, but suicide units, burrowers and jammers used sparingly.
- **Bosses:** not one per mission — varied missions, some destroy structures. Moving fortress: can't picture it in
  Arcline art. NO boss warning screen / big centre text. Atmosphere over notifications: a character on comms mentions
  the boss in passing.
- **World:** all yes. Also try spaceships, combat in space, on moons, other planets.
- **Modes:** campaign + contracts + missions first. Arena/PvP simple, maybe.
- **Progression:** all yes; mission grades affect rewards. Seasons on hold until the game succeeds. Salvage SIMPLE:
  clear low/mid/high tier materials, no piles of components (Voidwright is borderline too complex).
- **Co-op:** all yes. ALWAYS shared pay, never penalize playing together. Idea: repair drones / nanobots that heal,
  shield, stun.
- **Voice:** NOT our voice tools (too robotic). Wants a natural free voice option.
- **Presentation:** all yes. Damage numbers. Warframe-style crit tiers (over 100% crit chance → crits that crit), late game.

## Round 2 rulings (items 110–160)
- **Name: Overdrive Frame.**
- **Upgrades:** firmware chips in the CPU (yes), frame licences from makers (yes). NO pilot implants (the mech may be
  a drone / non-human).
- **Drones / healing:** one weapon mechanic among many. No tank/healer/damage trinity required. Game should be
  challenging, so healing has a niche.
- **Loot/crafting:** all yes. Basic recipes known from the start; more unlock with level/progress and show LOCKED in
  the panel; others are SECRET (hidden until learned) — secret crafts include odd weapons not otherwise obtainable,
  or crafted versions with an interesting tweak.
- **Crits/damage types/status:** yes. **Missions:** yes, loves orbital drop.
- **Places:** pick ONE environment to focus on first; others planned, minimal in v1.
- **Hub:** the hangar is a place you walk around with shops in it, not menus ("menus are lame").
- **Platform:** desktop + controller first; phone usable if possible, not a priority.
- **Rendering: FULL PRERENDERED for now** (no live 3D mixed in — owner saw the matching caveats).
- **City Kit:** reuse it for this game; add categories to filter by game/genre (Arcline's = "futuristic"/"neon").
- **New experiment page:** a mech part viewer / "dress up" page to check every part visually outside the game.
- **Voice:** on hold. Still have dialogue and cinematics (text); voice-over maybe later.

## Round 3 — one-at-a-time questions (2026-11-17)
1. First place: **neon city** (City Kit). Others planned, minimal in v1.
2. Co-op: **online rooms** (own server on games.radgh.com, up to 4, drop in/out, solo offline with bots).
3. Hub: **walk your own mech** around the hangar; stations are bays; friends' mechs are there too.
4. Mech look: **a different style per maker** (sleek, chunky, rounded, scrap-built); mixing makers looks odd on purpose.
5. Enemies v1: **alien swarm + a rival company's army** (army brings tanks, artillery, gunships, enemy mechs, aces).
6. Campaign: **city map of districts** held by the enemy; story missions push in; contracts appear in reached
   districts; the city visibly recovers; missions can chain.
7. Length: **mostly 4–8 min**, some 8–15 min missions that are built up to, a few 15+ min elaborate ones late game.
8. Aiming: **both** — free aim (mouse / right stick) with weapon lock-ons, plus a sticky lock-on button.
9. Losing: **fail, keep the loot** picked up; no pay/grade; mech never lost; never too punishing; checkpoint mid-way
   in long missions.
10. Co-op levels: **host's mission, fair rewards** — each player's loot/pay scaled to their own level, a newer
    player gets a gentle boost, story progress counts for everyone who finished.
11. Comms: **the speaker's machine** rendered small (mech / dropship / command drone) + callsign + moving waveform.

Claude's own calls: no Q/E view turning in v1 (owner finds it disorienting); buildings in front of a mech fade
see-through instead. Player mechs = layered baked parts (legs / core / arms / weapons baked separately, stacked at
run time; legs face movement, upper body faces aim; paint via a baked colour mask).
