Playground

Experiments and prototypes for future games. Each lives in its own folder with a README. This page is maintained by Claude whenever an experiment is added.

1. Voice Lab

Synthetic character voices in the browser, knob-driven. Compare engines (Web Speech, espeak, SAM, babble, Piper neural), tune pitch / speed / tone / texture knobs, apply audio effects, build presets, stress-test crowds, and export voice JSON.

audiottsweb-audio

2. Lingo

Text-based language system: phrase templates with {placeholders}, a game-defined dictionary (races, spells, items, people) with singular/plural/possessive forms, personality knobs, trait tags, custom catchphrases, and a two-character conversation simulator.

textdialogpersonality

3a. Avatar 2D

Paper-doll character builder from stacked SVG layers: hair, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, body height / width, tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories. Presets, randomize, save in browser, export JSON.

svgcharacter

3b. Avatar 3D

Three.js character builder. Chibi procedural body with a drawn face, plus a Quaternius (CC0) modular-mesh mode. Height / width, hair, outfits, idle + walk animation. Same JSON as the 2D version.

three.jscharacter

4. Name Forge

Procedural names for people, factions, regions, settlements, landmarks, artifacts and mottos across 12 races, each with its own language (sound rules + concept dictionary), meanings, plural/adjective forms and pronunciations. Exports Lingo dictionary entries.

nameslanguagestags

5. Item Vault

Tagged catalog of ~340 items (weapons, armour, vessels, regalia, lore, tools, instruments, food…) with race affinities and exclusives, plus a loot roller: material, quality, enchantment, artifact name, value and lore.

itemsloottags

Conversations

Back-and-forth talk with structure: topics with roles and requirements over real memories, loot, gear comparisons and weapon kill counts, spoken through Lingo personalities.

dialogmemory

Damage Meter

Drill-down combat meter: bars per fighter, drill into sources, drill into every hit with crit, overkill and damage type. Feed it from any combat system.

combatui

Asset Library

The drawn art both prototypes share, in files instead of code: 32 scenery backdrops (villages, forests, caves, volcanic plateaus, voids) with tags and a night wash, plus the map node icons. Browse them in a gallery, load them by id.

svgsceneryicons

Library

Blueprints of characters, NPCs, items and parties shared by every experiment and prototype. Stamp copies into games; export JSON or sync straight to Claude.

blueprintssync

Creatures 3D

Chibi procedural bodies for non-humanoids: wolves, boars, bears, rats, horses, deer, bats, spiders, snakes, drakes and dragons, with walk/attack/dead animations and a creature JSON.

3Dproceduralanimals

Vehicles 3D

Procedural travel vehicles in the creature style: hand cart, pack mule, covered wagon, ox cart, iron-plated war wagon, closed coach and a sled pulled by a drake — with real animal bodies in the shafts.

3Dproceduraltravel

Spell FX

Spell effects for a 3D stage: element projectiles (flame cones, ice shard clusters, jagged bolts, arcing runes), impact bursts and 23 looping status auras, built from shaped geometry and the fx sprite sheet.

3Dparticlescombat

Sound Lab

Sound effects with four interchangeable makers — our own synthesizer, a CC0 sample pack, a hybrid of the two, and chiptune — over one catalog of 118 logical ids (spells, statuses, melee, deaths, loot, interface, ambience). Every clip is measured and levelled, so nothing is too quiet or too loud.

audioweb-audioloudness

World Forge

Procedural world maps with a lot of knobs: seven ways to grow continents, erosion, rivers and lakes, climate and biomes, an evil/blessed aura layer, named regions, towns, dungeons and roads — then zoom from the world into a region and into a single 640 m tile.

worldgenmapnoise

Star Forge

A universe with a lot of knobs: a galaxy laid out in spiral arms, clusters or rings; eleven kinds of star from blue giants to black holes; systems of planets with moons, rings, belts and comets; fourteen planet archetypes with their own hazards and rare elements — then land on one and World Forge draws its surface.

universespace3dworldgen

Procedural Towns

The one true town planner, and a page to tune it on. A town is cut into blocks and the cuts become its streets, so a house can never sit on a road; each plot knows which way it faces, so a door is never on a blank back wall. Seven cultures — human, elf, dwarf, undead, orc, halfling, desert — are parameter sets over one generator. Farhold imports this module, so tuning here tunes the game.

townsprocgenlayout

Building Kit

The parts every building in a town is made of — walls, roofs, doors, windows, chimneys, stalls — drawn on their own so a roof that does not meet its walls is obvious here rather than three hundred metres away in the game. Same module the planner fills its plots with.

towns3dparts

Combined demo

One character sheet that ties the three together: an avatar with a voice and a personality that talks.

integration

Prototypes

Small throwaway games built from the experiments, to test ideas before they get their own project.

Party Quest

Assemble four adventurers, talk to townsfolk, take quests, travel by choosing directions, auto-battle with reactive banter, camp at night and listen to the party talk about the day.

prototypepartyauto-battler

Emberveil (rebuilt)

The user's Emberveil RPG rebuilt on the playground pieces: thirty classes as chibi characters with new gear parts, a branching world map across six acts, loot with affixes, uniques and sets, talents and passives, towns, events, dungeons.

prototyperpgloot

Frontier Foundry

A 4X + RTS automation game, playable end to end. Survey a star system, land a pod, scan for what is under the ground, put drills on it, haul it with delivery routes, refine it, place outlines that builders walk over and finish, research your way up an 82-node tree, hold off waves that arrive on a clock driven by your own smoke, and leave on a rocket carrying whatever fits in the hold. Six screens over a headless engine, and a 480-tile map that generates more ground around the camera as you pan.

prototype4Xautomationtower-defenceplayable

Farhold

A third-person RPG on a whole planet, and off it. Land on a world of a seeded star system, walk 163 km by 82 km of real elevation out to a horizon almost eight kilometres away, and fight, loot and level your way across it — with weather, rivers, roads, towns full of people who talk, four skills a class, jobs, a campaign and a nemesis. The star crosses a fifteen-minute sky and the neighbours are genuinely up there, where their orbits put them; press J and you can fly to one. All ten phases done.

prototyperpgplanet3dlootspace
