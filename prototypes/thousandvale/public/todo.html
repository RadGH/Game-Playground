Thousandvale: still to do

What's planned or known and not built yet. The game is paused while its maker works on other things, so nothing here has a date. Updated 2026-10-20. To see what has changed, open What's new on the title screen.

Found a bug or have an idea? Use the Feedback button in the game. Every report is read, and the ones that get built show up under What's new.

Built, coming with the next update

  • A new Torbor Downs. The first island is rebuilt with more varied ground: fourteen regions with their own look (chalk downs, oak woods, pine highwood, heather moor, Saint's Meadow, fen, red scarps, gorse heath, golden wolds, Blackthorn Scar, high fell, grey crags and a shingle shore), about twice as hilly with valleys and plains kept, and a real coastline. Every town and road is where it was; camps, curios and quest spots on ground that changed were moved. A character saved somewhere that is now sea or cliff wakes on the nearest safe ground.
  • Grass and trees. Thicker grass that reaches further and looks different in each region, and storybook trees that look the same close up as far away.
  • Achievements and trophies (press Y), with rewards by post, trophies for world bosses and sieges, and Realm Firsts. Link items, achievements and trophies in chat.
  • A new character screen with a calling picker that shows every skill, and a clear message for a name that's taken.
  • Town sizes (hamlet, village, town, city), every shop in every city, and the ox-cart window showing each stop's size and who works there.
  • Two-handed polearms (Glaive, Bardiche), two-spear attacks that use both hands, lightning that looks like lightning, Storm Orbs you can see.
  • Fixes from your reports: mounting refused with nothing near, a Vharlen dungeon that dropped you in the sea (and a /reset command), boss rewards that went missing, repeated event messages, the damage meter reopening, the map arrow on phones, barriers on the health bar, guards helping in a siege, a controller test page, and class descriptions that match the real skills.

Bigger jobs, waiting for a go-ahead

  • Rare gathering spots. Ore, wood and herb spots that can be magic, rare or legendary like fishing pools, giving more skill and more materials. Skinning takes its rarity from the beast.
  • More from the trades. Learn recipes one at a time, rework an item while you wear it, and add higher-tier gathering spots so gathering pays as well as questing at mid levels.
  • More of the world. Eleven more regions, each completely different from the last, reached by sea like Vharlen.
  • A better-looking world. More detailed props, livelier water with foam and drifting low cloud, softer lighting, and painted portraits in the unit frames.
  • New character models to replace the current ones, once every outfit, weapon and mount has been moved over.

Small things we know about

  • The Bound Trader on the road is "tied up" but there's no rope or post.
  • The inn sign and notice post have a stray stone step beside them.
  • You can walk through the bars of the Caged Scout's cage.
  • Selling items back pays very little (6% of their price). Raising it means re-balancing loot first.
  • The damage meter doesn't show overkill or absorbed damage for damage over time or thorns, and Wheeling Stars shows 0.
  • A shot stopped by a dungeon wall shows nothing where it stops.
  • Fishing has no "ready" moment like the other trade mini-games.
  • Port Vharl's jetty is decoration only, the town square is a walk from its pier, and the ship drops you just outside town.
  • Three of the four geysers in Fumarole Fen have no ledge to throw you onto.
  • On Vharlen, the hired carter still drives an ox cart and stables use the mainland's horseshoe sign.
  • Torbor Downs' curios and wayside stones don't appear in the world yet (you only get their story line).
  • On the new Torbor Downs, the Gorse March meets the sea in cliffs, so it has only two fishing spots.
  • Bug-report screenshots that include the interface only work in desktop browsers. Phones send the world view only.
  • Long shields lie sideways at the ready, and stiff skirts clip through the legs at a full stride.
