# Emberveil world notes (condensed from the research brief; raw data is in data/*.json built by tools/build-emberveil-data.mjs)

## Structure
Prologue (zone `prologue`, act 0) + 6 acts × 2 zones: border_roads, thornwood | dust_roads, ember_plateau | hell_breach, shattered_core | cosmic_rift, eternal_void | abyssal_depths, primordial_nexus | dragons_reach, dragon_throne. Zones are hand-authored node graphs (data/zones.json: nodes with id/type/name/x/y/exits + encounter | dialogEventId | shrineType | skillCheck). Node types: combat, dialog, town, treasure, ambush, boss, lore, shrine, challenge, dungeon, skillCheck.
Zone chain (zone-tables.json ZONE_UNLOCK_MAP): killing the zone's boss node unlocks the next zone. Act boss zones: prologue→act1, thornwood→2, ember_plateau→3, shattered_core→4, eternal_void→5, primordial_nexus→6. Fast travel: any visited node. Encounters have memory: cleared combat nodes stay cleared (revisit = 50% drop chance, no event).
Towns: one per act — 1 Emberglen (merchant, tavern, cleric), 2 Ashfort (+blacksmith, trainer), 3 Ironhold Bastion (+enchanter), 4 Starfall Haven (+black market), 5 The Last Bastion, 6 Drakehold. Guild hall when fame ≥ 500. Town `start` node of the first zone of each act is the town.
Start state: gold 150, act 1, border_roads/start, party from hire builder.

## Node handling
combat/ambush/challenge: encounter id → data/encounters.json (groups {ref,count,overrides}). boss: same + boss entity from bosses.json + boss-phases.json (deathDialog lines). dialog: dialog-events.json[dialogEventId] (lines, choices with effect {gold, startCombat, addItem, storyFlag, recruitHero…}, skillCheck {stat, dc} → outcomes pass/fail, outcomes {text, reward}). shrine: shrineType heal|buff|… (heal = full party heal, once). skillCheck: {stat, dc, flavor, success {gold,text}, failure {hpLoss,text}} (once). treasure: 60–119 gold once. lore: text (+ lore unlock). Random events (random-events.json, 166): picked for empty nodes by minLevel + zone + not seen; same shape as dialogs.
Skill check roll: attribute (best in party) + d20 ≥ dc.

## Enemies
data/enemies.json entities: {id, name, hp, maxHp, dmg[lo,hi], armor, hit, dodge, magicResist, blockChance?, xpValue, gold[lo,hi], spellList[], spellChance, statusOnHit?, role?}. Spells in enemy-spells.json {id, name, fxKind, cooldown, target single|aoe|ally_lowest_hp, effect {damage, heal, status…}, windUp?}. Bosses in bosses.json (+loot[] base ids). Scaling: see rules-notes.md (global ×0.58 hp / ×0.30 dmg, act multipliers, party-size dmg mult). Champions: 5% chance, +50% hp, +30% dmg, 1–2 modifiers (regen, aura_damage, fast, extra_strong, tough, cursed_aura…).

## Companions
Party cap: 4 heroes + 4 companions. Companion power P (1–5), cost = P×100, L = max(1, round(partyAvgLevel)); maxHp = round(30P + 8PL); dmg = [max(1, round(3+P+PL)), max(lo+1, round(5+2P+1.5PL))]. Attr bonus per level STR+1 DEX+1 CON+1.5 INT+0.5. Class pets from talents (druid wolf/bear, mage familiar, ranger war hound, necromancer skeleton/bone golem, warlock imp/hound, pyromancer fire elemental, stormcaller lightning elemental, tinker turret). Hires (tavern): borin warrior L1, aela ranger L1, rekk rogue L1, lysa cleric L2, ysolde_cogwright tinker L3, kaldrek/syra/vorin/maelis dragon_knight L6–9; cost max(100, level×100). Custom hire cost round(100×L×(1+0.1(L−1))). Recruitable story heroes: halden (fighter), bulwark_dorn (paladin), kessa_quill (ranger), sister_veya (cleric), magnus_orre (mage) via dialog outcomes.

## Dungeons (data/dungeons.json)
6 stages each (combat ×4, one skill_check, boss); pass skill check = enemies stunned round 1; fail = party loses 12–18% hp. Reward gold/xp/item (rare, high). Sealed after clearing. Min levels 3/6/9/12/14/16.
Infinite Depths (post act-5): floors, hp ×1.15^floor, dmg ×1.10^floor, anchor boss every 5 floors (+25% hp), floor effects (champion_tide, heal_disabled, double_damage, haste_enemies, volatile_ground, iron_skin, veil_rift, extra_group, blessed_loot, soul_drain), loot legendary chance min(0.6, 0.02+0.015 floor).

## Story (as shipped)
Prologue: wanderer meets goblin scouts, Silas Veilward (mentor), kills the Veilspawn Herald. Act I goblin frontier (Emberglen, Thornwood; bosses Warlord's Vanguard, The Veil Wardens; cast: Iris Vael, Sir Garrick Ostmere, Krix Bonechewer, Tomek Halverin, Mira the Seer). Act II Ashen Wastes (Veil cult; Lysa dead, letter accuses Silas; Marek Greel appears; Mother Yssira; bosses Lava Titan, Veil High Priest). Act III Hell Breach (Garrick's last stand; Silas confronted at Dreadhearth: spare/execute; bosses Archfiend Malgrath, Emberveil Sovereign; hidden Void Scholar). Act IV Cosmic Void (Kaela Thorne, void shards; bosses Void Herald, The Unraveler; hidden Echo of the Sovereign). Act V Primordial Abyss (Marek Greel IS the Architect; Yssira's sacrifice; bosses Primordial King, The Architect; hidden First Ember). Act VI Dragon's Reach (dragon court, Bahamorth the Dragon King final boss; Ancient Dragon zone boss).
Main quests mq_act1..5 (Blood at the Border, Embers Over Cinderhold, The Veil Breach, Shards of the Architect, The Architect) complete on boss kills border_boss, plateau_boss, breach_boss, core_boss, rift_boss.
NG+: ng+1, gold halved (min 150), +5 attr points each, enemies hp 4.5^ng, dmg 2.8^ng (boss ×1.35/×1.2), armor ×(1+0.55ng), hit +5, dodge +3 per ng; shop tiers +1.
