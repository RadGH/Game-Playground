Customize world

The planet
the number every world, town and road is grown from. The same seed and zone count always make the same planet
start on a settled world with several biomes, towns and trade. Turn this off and you start on the harshest world in the system instead — no towns and no trade — with a settled world 1 flight away. Either way the starting system always holds a settled world.
planet size is what changes how far away everything is: the hills, the ring of ground you can see and how fast the space ship flies all shrink with the planet
Levels and zones
how many level bands the same ground is cut into. This does not change the size of the planet — planet size does that
how many levels the whole game is cut into. The total experience is the same either way — a 1–100 world is the same climb in twice as many steps, so you level up about twice as often and each one gives half as much. Pair it with many zones for a level band every few minutes

How the levels are laid out

A zone is one level band, and the bands always tile the whole range of the world you are on with no gaps. A world's difficulty is the bottom 60%, the next 20% and the top 20% of the level range you picked — on the standard ladder that is 1–30 on a settled world, 30–40 out in the far reach and 40–50 in the deep dark. You always start on a settled world, and the system you start in always has somewhere you can breathe.
