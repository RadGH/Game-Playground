FARHOLD

Land on a world of a real star system, walk it to the horizon, kill what is out there, and wear what it drops. The other planets of the system are in the sky above you.

World settings
how many level bands the same ground is cut into — use planet size below to change how far a walk actually is
land on a settled, many-biomed world. Turn it off and you come down on the harshest rock in the system instead — no towns, no trade — with the blue world one short flight away. Either way the starting system always has one.
this is the one that changes how far away everything is: the hills, the ring of ground you can see, and how fast the ship flies all shrink with it

A zone is one level band, and the bands always tile the whole range of the world you are on with no gaps — 1–30 on a settled world, 30–40 out in the far reach, 40–50 in the deep dark. You always start on a 1–30 world, and the system you start in always has somewhere you can breathe.
