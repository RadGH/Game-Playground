> explosions and wildfires is cool. Make another game based on that, but keep it medieval fantasy with magic as the
> general theme. Keep it pixely but let's add tiny multi pixel units for larger things like pixel dragon that can fly
> around and breath fire. Let's have regular sandbox mode and a challenges mode with one act for now of just 6 levels.

> objectives to be like drop a meteor into a group of enemies (swarm of pixels), or wall off a flood of lava, survive

> detailed RTS features, keep it a simple physics sandbox world. I think we should focus on fun and add lots of
> explosions, reactions, etc. Besides sandbox mode there should be a mana system that regenerates. Some incremental
> gameplay features. Drip feed new features. Lots of units to place, materials to build with, and spells to cast. Use
> colorful lighting and other effects so everything looks really pretty and colorful. Design interesting systems for
> level generation, progression, and expand on the features I've described. Create a new game with a new name. It is
> not directly related to any previously created games besides the reference I already provided. I especially want to
> play it on my phone so ensure it is very mobile friendly and easy to navigate spells and other features.
