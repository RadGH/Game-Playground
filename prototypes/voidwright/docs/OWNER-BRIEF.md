# Owner brief (verbatim, 2026-11-06)

Create a new 2d space game with colorful lighting and shockwaves and beaming lasers. You design your own ship by placing blocks as hull, defense, offense, utility nodes, and other parts. You can equip a large variety of weapons that have different targeting modes like automated laser turrets, homing missile launcher, manual beams, dum fire rockets, lightning weapons, energy shields that block a direction or bubble the entire ship. You go around mining asteroids for resources to build parts. You can get dronebay and build a variety of drones with their own weapons that travel nearby the player, not behind you but up front ready for action. Different materials have different properties and the difficulty level increases as you travel further increasing the quality of drops, difficulty of enemy, availability at shops. Have a variety of shops with things to do well beyond what I've described here and take initiative to expand this idea 500%. There should be a build mode with indicators of where a turret can aim and rotation important to cover your back in some cases. Armor blocks that absorb damage. Broken blocks can be repaired out of combat. An economy that lets you buy low and sell high with clear UI that makes it easy to be a trader. Mining lasers to harvest small asteroids or planets. Nearby a planet you can safely hover above the ground and shoot downwards, no need to land on surfaces in this game. Ability to buy new ships which come with predesigned layouts. Different scale ships available as you progress in the game eventually unlocking frigates, battleships, destroyers, and other class ships which feature different parts, larger blocks, and different gameplay. Fights against several small ships or one big one. Armor types that must be balanced to defend against small or large ships, cannons or machine guns, lasers or plasma. The hull should be built using blocks but once completed should add decorations so that wedges become slopes and creates a more natural look something like how HQ4x makes pixel games look smooth. Turrets should have their own graphics, rotate at enemies, and have cosmetic knockbavk depending on type. The game should drip feed new features rather than release them all at once which would be overwhelming. There should be a simple to follow quest that leads you through the game but is open form so you don't have to follow any specific routes or go to any specific location, more vague goals that can be accomplished anywhere. Implement a crew system and CPU capacity that can both be upgraded and are used for different weapons or tools. Create a harpoon that can latch on to an enemy or asteroid and actually let you pull it around until you detach. Ships that get destroyed should eject resources as well as escape pods containing crew. Add alien and drone enemies that don't offer crew but offer more varying non human rewards. Generate a few races with their own themes and weaponry.

The core of the game is build your ship > kill stuff or work for stuff > upgrade your ship > kill bigger stuff.

The scale between ships should be similar to EVE or Spore where you start tiny, fighting tiny ships, but stations are massive. Later you end up in a huge ship 100x bigger than your starting ship that is immensely more powerful. You can fight these early in if you look hard enough but you should be basically disadvantaged.

Ensure there are a variety of weapon types with detailed and interesting graphics, loss of secondary effects, wide variety of sfx.

No need for the on screen report bug button, change your memory to move that to the options menu.

Destroyed ships should have physics, satisfying explosions, rarity colored loot with awesome effects like diablo 2 / 3 but spaces themed.
