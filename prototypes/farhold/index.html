FARHOLD

Land on a world of a real star system, walk it to the horizon, kill what is out there, and wear what it drops. The other planets of the system are in the sky above you.

Phase 2 of ten. No sound and no dialogue yet. WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, left click to swing, I for the character sheet, and ` (backtick) for the debug menu — where you can change the weather, the time of day and how thick the world is.
