**§10 "Decisions from the first reconciliation pass"** now settle those disagreements. Your job is to make
your assigned files agree with canon and with the owner pages named in §10 — **edit, do not rewrite**.

Rules:
1. Read `00-OVERVIEW.md` in full (especially §4 and §10) and `_BRIEF.md`.
2. For each ruling, look up the owner page's actual table when you need the detail (e.g. page 02's key
   table, page 07's unlock ladder, page 08's slots and currencies, page 10's warband bands, page 12/13's
   boss ids).
3. Change only facts that conflict: keys, unlock levels, slot names, currency ids/names, warband levels,
   quest id patterns, flying mounts, boss ids, colours. Keep every other word. Keep numbers you are not told
   to change.
4. Where a page lists "canon change requests" or "questions" that §10 has now answered, mark them
   **Resolved (00 §10)** in place rather than deleting them.
5. Never introduce a new conflict. If a fix needs a decision §10 does not make, do not guess — list it in
   your report.
6. Documentation only. No game code. Do not edit files you were not assigned.
7. Final report: per file, a short bullet list of what you changed, plus anything still unresolved.
