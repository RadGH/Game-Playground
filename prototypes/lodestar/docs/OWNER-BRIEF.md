# Owner brief (Radley, 2026-10-28) — verbatim

> Create a pixel physics rts where you try to build a city while also building walls and towers to defend against enemies similar to Factorio: spawners that send raiding parties. Walls should be easy to draw and connect contiguous lines with multiple tiers starting with wooden walls and reinforcing to be stronger. Produce units that can be set on patrol. The map must be discovered to reveal the fog of war and at the start only surface level resources are detected with a limited quantity. You have to mine each resource and have the materials shipped back to base to refine into bars or other materials, which should all be automatable. As you progress through research you unlock better defenses and travel mechanisms and eventually new delivery methods like cars, trains, or boats. All delivery methods actually have too travel and are in danger of enemies attack so you have to defend them, however trains and such should have certain protection: rails can't be hurt as they aren't offensive and the train car is fast enough to outrun enemies or even plow through them. The player can also activate different spells that allow them to interact with the world by tossing meteors, creating shield bubbles, drawing a line of fire, or zapping creatures with lightning. These draw mana from HQ that is harvested by crystals and had to be processed before it can be used. Mana batteries let you increase storage and unlock better spells. From there the game becomes incremental n terms of economy, combat, etc. Through research you eventually can launch into space and send satellites to nearby planets and scan them. Scanning is like Kerbal space program mods where it scans at the orbit line but because the planet is spinning it eventually reveals everything though it may take several planetary days. Different probes can detect diffeeent material. Rockets deliver material to space and has to be related back to HQ using a reliable and reusable automated transport system between space stations, one per planet, supplied by delivery vehicles. The first system should always have 6-10 planets. Eventually you'll unlock a warp drive that can be built at a space station and allows travel to a nearby start system. Eventually you'll have trade routes between star systems. Resources including mana are planet specific but creating a base in the surface of another planet comes with a set of starting materials and a small mana battery. Mana should slowly regenerate but ultimately requires mana crystal. Mana crystal also used as a resource to craft magical energy sources and empowered units. Different planets should have different and more dangerous enemies, but also useful high tier resources. Everything in the game is represented in pixels with lighting effects such that a spark emits light into pixels nearby and may be a bump map system so that tall pixels light up more for example. Trains and walls may be seamless lines and curves rather than pixels. The goal is not to micro manage units but to station them at permanent locations or on patrol paths. Do a brain storming session to expand these ideas. I want the game to feel like I'm sitting at command HQ acting as commander, not like a floating god above the planet. I want to interface with actual screens, knobs, and buttons to interact with menus. The interface should appear as physical machines I'm using to view and interact with the world in an abstract way. Bonus points for satisfying explosions, lava and water physics, and making good use of the pixel physics system. It's OK to stay beyond my written up doc if it creates a better game. This is your game, built for me.

Name chosen by Claude: **Lodestar** (working title).
