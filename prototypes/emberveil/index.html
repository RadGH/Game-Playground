Emberveil 2

A dark-fantasy party RPG: hire heroes from thirty classes, walk a branching world map three nodes a day, keep the party fed, fight turn-based auto-battles, rest by a fire where the party talks about the day, and chase loot with affixes through six acts.

Every class is a Mii-style character with its own gear parts. Beasts use the creature bodies. Talk comes from the Lingo engine, voices from the Formant synth. Save any hero to the character library.

Build your party

Pick a class (all thirty are open here; the original's unlock rule is shown on each card). Each hero starts with the class kit, its first skill, and attributes spent by its build preset.
